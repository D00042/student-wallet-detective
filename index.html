What the data says

    Method, definitions and data note

    Data. All 640 purchases are generated in this page by a seeded random generator (seed 2026), so the same data appears on every visit. It is not real student data and is not connected to a database.

    Assumptions built into the generator. Weekend food and entertainment weights are 1.8 times higher. September university and shopping weights are 1.9 times higher. Amounts are log-normal around a median per category, with small multipliers by city and study level. The findings therefore describe these assumptions. This page demonstrates the analysis and is not a research result.

    Definitions. Total spend is the sum of purchase amounts. Mean is total divided by purchases. Median is the middle purchase; it is less affected by a few large purchases. Weekend share is weekend spend divided by total spend, compared with weekend days as a share of calendar days in the selected period.

    Use your own data. Replace the generator in the script with rows of the same shape: date, city, level, category, amount.
